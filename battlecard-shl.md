## TL;DR for the 2-minute pre-call read

SHL is the assessment industry's validity library: 1,000+ instruments, public technical manuals, 300+ people scientists, 45M+ assessments a year, 80% of the FTSE 100. Concede all of it — then reframe. Their science lives in separate instruments (OPQ personality, Verify cognitive, **MQ.M5 motivation — yes, they have one; do NOT claim they don't measure motivation**) that need expert synthesis; competitor reviewers' favorite contrast is scores that "don't require expert interpretation." Their most-documented weakness is candidate-facing: dated UX, stacked test batteries, translation complaints — and their own November 2025 research hands you the quote (59% of US workers say AI worsens hiring bias; 74% judge the company by the assessment experience) while SHL Labs builds an AI interviewer. They are 8+ years into an Exponent PE hold with flat headcount (~2,465, 54% in India) — renewals are margin events. Zero Role product (they sold their US gov assessment-consulting arm PDRI to Pearson in 2023), zero Experience product, and a 15-month-old Talent Mobility product is their first real move past hiring. **Fight on funnel yield, activation, and the Role layer — never on validity.**

## 2. Positioning + narrative

- **One-liner:** "People science. People answers." — the science-and-scale incumbent. Criteria they install: published validity, norm depth (30+ countries, 37 sectors, 5 job levels), instrument breadth, EEOC-aligned documentation, 45B+ datapoints, 35+ languages. [Documented, cached]
- **Criteria they avoid:** candidate completion/drop-off rates, time-to-insight without expert interpretation, integrated motivation-in-decision frames, anything Role or Experience, transparent pricing.
- **Trajectory story:** pushing beyond hiring — Smart Interview Professional (Feb 2025, AI question generation + real-time interviewer prompts), **Talent Mobility (Apr 2025)** — skills data + predictive analytics for redeployment. This narrows but does not close the activation gap: it is dashboards from an assessment factory, 15 months old. [Documented, cached]
- **AI posture — the usable irony:** SHL Labs is prototyping a generative-AI "digital human" interviewer while SHL's own Nov 17, 2025 "AI Trust Gap" research (1,000+ US workers) reports only 27% fully trust employer AI use, **59% say AI worsens bias, 74% say an AI-agent interview changes their perception of the company**. Their research is your discovery script. [Documented, cached]
- **Precision note (propagate everywhere):** SHL **does** sell a Motivation Questionnaire — MQ.M5, 18 dimensions, ~20 min, current catalog. The safe attack is integration, not existence: MQ is a standalone add-on to buy and interpret on top of OPQ, not an integrated Want×Can frame where motivation and capability are read together in one decision.

## 4. Feature gaps (ranked for a 500–20K US buyer)

1. **Instrument silo vs integrated decision frame.**
   (a) Evidence: OPQ, MQ, Verify are separate catalog items; analyst comparisons repeatedly praise rivals' scores as "not requiring expert interpretation" — the contrast is drawn against SHL; services attach assumed in enterprise programs.
   (b) Confidence: high on product structure; medium on interpretation-dependency (two corroborating analyst sources).
   (c) Exploitation: "When your hiring manager opens the report, can they act on it that day — or does it go to a consultant queue first?" TMA reads Want and Can together in one frame; SHL sells you the pieces.

2. **Candidate experience / funnel yield.**
   (a) Evidence: documented complaints — dated interface, legacy tech, translation quality, convoluted item wording; multi-module batteries stack; no game-based sub-hour integrated experience found; their own 2025 research says 74% of candidates judge the company by the assessment experience.
   (b) Confidence: medium (some complaints may predate refreshes — date-check before quoting verbatim).
   (c) Exploitation: propose a head-to-head pilot on completion rate, drop-off, time-to-complete, candidate NPS. Never argue validity; argue yield.

3. **Role layer confirmed absent.**
   (a) Evidence: zero job-evaluation, grading, architecture, or pay-equity product found; PDRI divested 2023; searches surface only third-party vendors.
   (b) Confidence: high (well-searched absence).
   (c) Exploitation: pay-transparency deadlines land in the Total Rewards office where SHL has no product and no contact. Land STYR there; attach Person + Fit later.

4. **Activation gap — measurement without build.**
   (a) Evidence: development products are dashboards + virtual exercises; Talent Mobility is 15 months old from an assessment-factory DNA, sold by an HP/Microsoft-trained sales org.
   (b) Confidence: high on product facts; medium on the framing.
   (c) Exploitation: "After the assessment lands, who sits with the manager and builds the development plan?" TMA's consultative practice is the product, not a feature.

5. **Mid-market service arbitrage.**
   (a) Evidence: $25K–50K/yr mid-market ranges (second-hand); analysts route cost-sensitive buyers to alternatives; 500–5K orgs get the low-touch tier or a program sized for someone else; enterprise complexity/integration lift flagged.
   (b) Confidence: medium (pricing figures are search-extract ranges, not verified quotes).
   (c) Exploitation: named-practitioner service at mid-market price vs a portal login and a support queue.

6. **PE-clock economics.**
   (a) Evidence: 8+ year Exponent hold; flat headcount; India-weighted cost base — margin management posture, renewals are where margin gets made.
   (b) Confidence: high on facts; the posture is inference — frame as questions, never assertions.
   (c) Exploitation: coach champions to run the stack in parallel entering renewal season, when SHL is most rigid on price and least flexible on service.

**They win:**

- Litigation-hardened selection at scale: when the buyer's counsel wants the thickest validity file in the industry, SHL's library is the safe answer.

**Run-alongside assessment:** clean coexistence paths exist. ## 11. MEDDPICC angles

- **Metrics:** completion rate, candidate drop-off, time-to-complete, candidate NPS, time-from-report-to-action; cost per completed assessment incl. services attach. Bad hire ≈ 3x salary anchors the stakes.
- **Economic buyer:** CHRO/VP TA holds the SHL contract; CFO cares about the services line and renewal escalation; Total Rewards is a fresh buyer SHL never meets.
- **Decision criteria (reshape):** insert candidate experience, same-day interpretability, integrated motivation-in-decision, and Role-layer coverage into the evaluation matrix — four criteria the incumbent structurally fails.
- **Decision process:** run the pilot inside one job family so the data, not the RFP, makes the argument.
- **Identify pain:** funnel leakage in high-volume hiring; reports sitting uninterpreted; pay-transparency deadlines with no vendor support.
- **Champion:** the TA leader losing candidates mid-battery; the L&D leader whose development budget buys dashboards; the Total Rewards lead facing transparency laws alone.
- **Competition:** expect the validity-library speech and the safe-choice frame. Pre-empt by concede-and-reframe (§10) before they present.
