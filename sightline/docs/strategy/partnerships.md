# Partnerships: Five Priority Conversations

Format per partner: what they want / the ask / the give. Order matters; (a) is a prerequisite for Shield, (b) is the growth channel, (c) and (d) are the CAC subsidy, (e) is the endgame option.

## (a) Wholesale monitoring: Becklar/AvantGuard first, then COPS, Rapid Response

Becklar/AvantGuard is headquartered in Ogden, Utah, local to Ryan. That makes it THE first call: a drive, not a pitch deck, and a relationship before a contract.

- **What they want:** volume commitment and clean signals. Wholesale centers price on account count and hate false-alarm load.
- **The ask:** acceptance of the Guardian AVS-01 payload (scored, evidence-backed events; Guardian demos AVS-01 level 3 on the flagship scenario) as the handoff format, plus startup pricing near the COPS SecureNet anchor of $5.99/account/mo.
- **The give:** events that arrive pre-scored and pre-verified, in a market where 94 to 98% of dispatches are false industry-wide and Seattle ignored 96% of 13K false alarms in 2023. We are the cheapest accounts they will ever monitor. COPS Monitoring (SecureNet bundle, $5.99/account/mo) and Rapid Response are the parallel tracks and the pricing leverage. No need to build a UL central station, ever.

## (b) Lighting dealer networks: Trimlight, Gemstone, JellyFish

- **What they want:** year-round revenue. Permanent lighting sales are seasonal; dealers want recurring income and a reason to revisit 170K+ installed homes. They will ask for margin and exclusivity windows.
- **The ask:** access to their installed base for SKU 1 retrofits, attach at point of sale for new installs, and co-branding on the security tier.
- **The give:** 20 to 30% plan revenue share, tiered by attach volume, plus the $250-CAC attach motion that turns their service calls into commissions. Trimlight alone is 120K+ homes and 300+ dealers; its 2025 PE backing means the network now answers to owners who like RMR (see (e)). Exclusivity: offer metro-level windows of 6 to 12 months (est.) tied to attach quotas, never perpetual.

## (c) An insurer (State Farm template)

- **What they want:** loss-reduction data and claims-impact reporting. State Farm put $1.2B into ADT (plus Google's $450M+) precisely for predict-and-prevent; the appetite is proven at billion-dollar scale.
- **The ask:** premium-discount listing for SightLine homes and a $150 subsidized-CAC channel (modeled in econ/, the 6.5x LTV:CAC channel).
- **The give:** the predict-and-prevent data spec (partnerships/kit/), delivered under the privacy policy: aggregate, consented, claims-relevant event data. Core-tier homes send nothing (no cloud path exists), and that line is not negotiable; the insurer program rides on Plus/Shield opt-in only.

## (d) A builder (Lennar template)

- **What they want:** turnkey attach price, zero warranty headaches, and reliability. Lennar already preinstalls Ring in new builds; the slot on the spec sheet exists.
- **The ask:** the 48V rail as a standard or option-package line item across a community.
- **The give:** construction-stage install of the 48V rail, when eave access costs nothing, at a fixed per-home price with the 4% modeled callback rate (vs 11% old model) as the reliability number. The buyer gets Core $0 forever on day one, which is a sales-office talking point no alarm contract can match, and upgrades to Plus/Shield are pure attach upside.

## (e) Incumbent-lighting PE owners: channel now, acquirer later

Trimlight took PE backing in 2025. PE owners of lighting networks bought a seasonal install business and want recurring revenue multiples. RMR portfolios trade at ~26x to 50x monthly RMR (Barnes); a lighting network that attaches SightLine converts one-time install revenue into an RMR portfolio.

- **What they want:** multiple expansion on the asset they already own.
- **The ask:** network-wide dealer enablement today; keep the relationship warm as the natural acquirer or majority channel partner at scale.
- **The give:** the attach economics from (b), plus the roadmap view. Sequencing: treat (e) as a channel conversation for the first 18 months (est.); an early acquisition conversation caps the outcome, a late one prices off our portfolio at 35x RMR (modeled in econ/).
