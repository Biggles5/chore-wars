# Audio Recording Consent Matrix: 50 States + DC

CAVEAT: Informational only. Not legal advice. Laws change and caselaw shifts; verify with counsel before enabling audio recording anywhere. SightLine ships with audio OFF on every node, every SKU, every plan. This matrix drives the in-app warning shown when an owner attempts to enable audio, keyed to the install state. Where a state is mixed or unsettled, the app treats it as all-party.

Scope: consent rules for audio recording of oral communications (state wiretap/eavesdropping statutes). Video-only recording is governed separately. Federal law (18 U.S.C. 2511) is one-party; the stricter state rule controls.

## Product Behavior

- Audio is OFF by default everywhere. No exceptions.
- Enabling audio in an all-party, mixed, or unsettled state triggers a blocking in-app warning with the state-specific rule and requires typed owner acknowledgment.
- Outdoor mics capture people who have not consented. Even in one-party states, recording a conversation the owner is not part of can be illegal eavesdropping. The warning says so in every state.
- Neighbor-facing and street-facing zones keep audio disabled regardless of owner setting.
