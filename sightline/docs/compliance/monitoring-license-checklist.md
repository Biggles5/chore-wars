# Monitoring license checklist by state (Shield tier)

Alarm monitoring is state-licensed. The wholesale-station strategy (COPS / Rapid Response / Becklar class) carries most of the licensing load: the station holds the monitoring licenses and dispatch relationships. SightLine still has obligations as the seller/provider in several states. Informational, not legal advice; licensing counsel confirms before Shield activates in each state.

## The model

- The wholesale central station monitors and dispatches under its own state licenses.
- SightLine sells equipment and the Shield service. In some states that alone requires an alarm-company or contractor registration even with monitoring outsourced.
- Class 2 low-voltage self-install/dealer-install avoids electrical-contractor licensing in most jurisdictions, but a handful regulate "alarm system installers" separately.

## Checklist (per launch state)

| Step | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm the wholesale station is licensed for the state | station partner | ask for license numbers per state, keep on file |
| Determine whether SightLine needs an alarm company registration | counsel | states with company-level requirements (est. list below) |
| Determine installer licensing for low-voltage alarm work | counsel + ops | affects dealer certification requirements per state |
| Register local alarm permits process into homeowner onboarding | product | many cities require a homeowner alarm permit; the app should surface it at Shield activation |
| False-alarm ordinance mapping | product | fine schedules and verified-response rules feed Guardian's handoff policy per city |
| Contract review: no-contract terms vs state alarm-contract statutes | counsel | several states regulate alarm contract terms; ours are friendlier than required, verify anyway |

## States needing early attention (est., verify with counsel)

- **Licensing-heavy for alarm companies**: California (ACO), Texas (DPS Private Security), Florida, New York, New Jersey, Virginia, North Carolina, Tennessee, Nevada, Utah (DOPL burglar alarm licensing: home state, do it first).
- **Installer-level licensing common**: California, Texas, Florida, New Jersey, Oklahoma, Arkansas.
- **Verified-response cities to map for Guardian policy**: Seattle WA, Salt Lake City UT (verified response history), Las Vegas NV, Detroit MI, San Jose CA, Fremont CA, Aurora CO.

## Sequencing

Utah first (home market, DOPL scope confirmed with counsel), then the dealer-launch states in market-share-plan.md order. No Shield activation in a state before this checklist closes for it; Core and Plus (no monitoring) have no such gate.
