Dear Committee Members,

I am requesting approval to install (or, if the lighting track is already approved and installed, to add nodes within) a SightLine roofline system at the property above. The system consists of a low-voltage permanent lighting track mounted under the eave, matched to the trim color, with small sensor modules seated entirely inside the track channel.

**Daytime invisibility.** There are no visible camera housings, domes, brackets, or wires anywhere on the home. Each node is a 30mm module recessed inside the same track profile the committee sees on any permanent lighting installation. From the street and from neighboring lots, the installation is indistinguishable from lighting-only track. Photographs from 30 feet are attached.

**Lighting behavior.** Day-to-day, the track provides warm architectural downlighting consistent with community standards ([SPECIFY: e.g., 2700K warm white, no animation, curfew per HOA rules]). Holiday or color lighting will be used only as permitted by [COMMUNITY NAME] guidelines.

**Privacy commitments.** The system enforces privacy masks on the device itself: neighboring homes, neighboring yards, and windows are masked by default and are never recorded. Audio is disabled by default, and no street-facing audio will be enabled. Nothing about this installation observes any property other than my own.

**Electrical and safety.** The system is NEC Class 2 low voltage. No electrician, panel work, or exterior conduit is required. Certification: [ETL LISTING NUMBER / STATUS].

**Precedent.** Permanent roofline lighting tracks of this same form factor are already installed and approved in many communities, including installations by national brands across more than 170,000 homes. This request adds no visible elements beyond what those approvals cover. [IF APPLICABLE: This community has previously approved permanent lighting at ADDRESSES/LOTS.]

I am happy to provide a physical sample of the track and module, or to walk the committee through the attached photos on site. Thank you for your consideration.
