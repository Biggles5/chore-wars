# SightLine: Perimeter Intelligence

## The Category

Permanent roofline lighting became a category (Trimlight, Gemstone, JellyFish, Oelo, EverLights, Celebright, Govee). Security cameras became a commodity. Nobody merged them. SightLine puts camera nodes invisibly inside the lighting track homeowners already want on their house, adds reactive light deterrence, and narrates verified events with an AI that never fabricates imagery. That is a new category: Perimeter Intelligence. The house watches, verifies, and defends itself, invisibly.

## The Wedge

No incumbent ships a track camera. Not one. Trimlight (120k+ homes, 300+ dealers, PE-backed), JellyFish (50k+ homes), Gemstone, Oelo, EverLights, Celebright, Govee: all lights only. Trimlight's install base alone is 120k+ retrofit-ready rooflines with power already on the eave. The channel that sold those homes their lights can sell them their eyes, and we are the only product they can sell.

## Why Now

- Permanent lighting is exploding and PE money is consolidating dealers. The channel is being built for us, by someone else's capital.
- Cameras are commoditized. The sensor is cheap; the placement, verification, and channel are the product.
- Courts are rejecting AI-enhanced footage (State v. Puloka, 2024). Native-pixel evidence becomes a feature you must design for. We did.
- Police are done with noise: 94 to 98% of alarms are false, and Seattle now ignores unverified calls (96% of 13k calls false in 2023). Verified events are the only events that matter.
- Consumers are primed to defect: Vivint's $20M FTC settlement, contract resentment, battery cams dying every ~3 weeks, and 72% of consumers with privacy concerns (Parks).

## The Product

Two SKUs, one system.

- SKU 1, Retrofit: nodes drop into existing lighting tracks. $1,299 to $1,799 installed (est.).
- SKU 2, 48V Rail: new installs on our powered track, under 3 hours vs 8.5 today (installs today run 6 to 10 crew-hours with labor ~30% of $2,000 to $8,000 jobs).

Each node carries dual sensors: 8MP ~110 degree wide channel plus a 12MP ~60 degree identity channel, factory-aimed from a scan of the home. An eye-level door node covers what eave optics cannot. Deterrence is a whole-house zone-follow light strobe in under 1 second. 72-hour on-node loop. Audio off by default, privacy masks enforced on device and mandatory at setup. NEC Class 2 power, no electrician.

## The Moat

1. Scan-derived identity aiming: the 12MP channel is pointed by math from the home scan, per house, at factory. Competitors bolting cameras to eaves get wide context and useless faces.
2. Evidence-grade verified events: native pixels only, AI narrates but never fabricates. Built for the courtroom and the dispatcher, not the highlight reel.
3. The dealer channel: 300+ Trimlight dealers and thousands of Christmas-light installers already own the roofline and the homeowner's trust. We arm them; incumbents would have to disarm them.

## Business Model

Hardware margin on both SKUs, then software: Core is $0 forever with nothing leaving the house (no cloud path exists on Core). Plus $9.99/mo. Shield $19.99/mo. No contracts, ever. That is the anti-Vivint position, and it is why the 72% with privacy concerns can say yes.
