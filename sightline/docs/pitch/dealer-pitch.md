## Objection Handling

**"Cameras are a liability."**
SightLine is privacy-first by architecture. Masks are enforced on the device and mandatory at setup: neighbor windows and yards are never captured, not cropped after the fact. Audio ships OFF everywhere. Core plan has no cloud path at all, so there is nothing to subpoena from us and nothing to breach. You are selling the most defensible camera on the market, not the most exposed.

**"I don't want the support burden."**
The support agent triages every ticket before a human sees it, and the gateway self-heals: it detects a failed node, reroutes, and orders the replacement. Your obligation is a truck roll only when hardware is physically dead, and the margin covers it. Compare that to your current warranty calls on controllers.

**"Homeowners already have Ring."**
Ring sits at the door and sees a chest at 8 feet. Eave-height optics fail at identification: wrong angle, wrong resolution, wrong lens. SightLine's identity channel is aimed math: a 12MP ~60 degree sensor factory-pointed from the home scan at the approach paths, plus an eye-level door node. And when something happens, SightLine produces a verified event police act on, not one of the 94 to 98% of alarms they now ignore. Ring is a doorbell. This is a perimeter.
