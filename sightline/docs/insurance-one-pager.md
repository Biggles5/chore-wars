# SightLine for Insurers: Verified Perimeter Partnership

## The problem you price every day
Residential alarm data is noise. 94 to 98% of alarm calls are false, so "has an alarm" is a weak underwriting signal, and burglary claims arrive with no usable evidence: no suspect imagery, no timeline, no proof the system was even on.

## What SightLine changes
SightLine is a permanent roofline system: camera nodes embedded in decorative LED track, covering the full perimeter, with reactive light deterrence (the house lights follow the intruder).

1. Fewer incidents, documented. Zone-follow strobe deterrence turns most approaches into departures, and every deter event is logged with outcome. Deterrence stops being a claim; it becomes a data point.
2. Verified events only. The gateway correlator assembles Event Stories marked verified only on identify-grade capture or multi-node confirmation. A SightLine incident record is not "motion detected," it is a confirmed entity with a timeline.
3. Evidence-grade records. Exportable stories contain native-pixel frames only (never synthetic or interpolated imagery) with a chain of custody log. That is a claims file an adjuster and a prosecutor can both use.
4. Proof of liveness. Nodes publish health telemetry every 30 s. At any incident timestamp, telemetry proves the system was powered, online, and recording. No more "was the alarm actually armed" disputes.

## Proposed program

- Premium discount: 5 to 15% homeowner discount (est.) for active SightLine sites, mirroring the water-leak sensor programs already run by major carriers. Tier the discount by plan (Core, Plus, Shield with professional monitoring).
- Policy verification API: opt-in endpoint answering one question, "does this address have an active, healthy SightLine perimeter," backed by live telemetry. Verified at bind and at renewal, not self-reported.
- Claims package export: one-click export of the incident story (timeline, entity classes, deter outcome, native frames, chain of custody, liveness telemetry) delivered to the carrier on the policyholder's authorization. Cuts adjuster time and fraud exposure in the same motion.

## Why this beats crediting a doorbell camera
Doorbell and floodlight cameras cover one approach, are frequently offline, and produce clips of variable integrity. SightLine covers the entire roofline perimeter, is permanently installed and powered, self-reports its health every 30 s, and separates verified incidents from noise before anyone sees an alert.
