# SightLine x Carrier: Predict and Prevent, Verified

## The problem you price every day
94-98% of alarm dispatches in this industry are false. Seattle ignored 96% of 13,000 false calls in 2023. Unverified alarms do not prevent losses, they train everyone to ignore them. Your book still absorbs the burglary, vandalism, and package-theft claims.

## What SightLine is
Security camera nodes hidden inside permanent roofline LED tracks. No visible cameras. An agentic monitoring brain (Guardian) scores every event on the AVS-01 scale (TMA standard, ANSI 2023, IACP-ratified Oct 2024, 0-4). SightLine forwards only verified events: score >= 2 with video verification. Guardian never calls 911 and never asserts a crime; a human operator holds dispatch authority.

## Why this cuts your losses
1. Verified perimeter coverage reduces burglary, vandalism, and package-theft frequency and severity.
2. Telemetry proves the system was live at the incident timestamp. No more "was it armed" disputes at claim time.
3. Deterrence is documented with outcomes. Every deter event logs fled or stayed, so prevention becomes measurable, not anecdotal.
4. Claims that do occur come with evidence: AVS-01 scored story, native frames, chain of custody.

## The program we propose
- Premium discount program: 5-15% (est.) for active Shield or Plus policyholders, tiered by verified coverage.
- Opt-in policy verification API: carrier confirms system-live status for a consenting policyholder, anonymized signals only.
- Claims package export: police-readable narrative, story window, native frames, chain of custody, delivered on policyholder request.
- Privacy by design: masks enforced on-device, audio off by default, no face databases, insurer data limited to anonymized loss-relevant signals. Core-plan homes have no cloud path and are never in scope.
