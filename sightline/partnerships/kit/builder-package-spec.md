# SightLine Builder Package Spec

Template: the Lennar/Ring preinstall playbook, upgraded. State Farm put $1.2B into ADT; Lennar preinstalls Ring on every home. Builders already know security preinstalls sell. SightLine gives you the version with zero visible cameras.

## The numbers
- Per-home cost target: $900-1,100 builder net (est.) vs $1,299+ retail. The spread is your amenity margin or your closing incentive.
- Construction-stage labor: under 2 crew-hours per home during construction (est.) vs 2.4 job-hours retrofit.
- Reliability commitments: 3-year node warranty, telemetry-backed SLA, modeled 90-day callback rate <5%.

## Construction-stage install (why it is cheaper in your workflow)
1. Rough-in at fascia stage: 48V Class 2 rail mounts to fascia before paint, homerun to the controller location. Low voltage, never mains, no electrician trade dependency.
2. Nodes clip at finish stage: factory-cut kit per plan/elevation, no measuring, no cutting, no splicing on site.
3. Commissioning before closing: 30-point check (power 6, mechanical 6, optics 6, privacy 4, network+system 5, handoff 3), run by a certified crew, QC photo gates archived per lot.

## Turnkey provisioning at closing
- Scripted homeowner app handoff at the closing walkthrough: owner PIN set, privacy masks confirmed on-device, audio confirmed off, plan choice presented (Core $0 local-only, Plus $9.99, Shield $19.99 with station handoff).
- Buyer leaves closing with a working system, not a coupon.
- SightLine handles activation, monitoring, warranty, and support. Your warranty desk never sees it.

## Model-home demo program
- Every model home gets a live demo rig: the 2:14 AM story replays on command from the sales agent's tablet. Lights escalate, verified event appears, no camera visible anywhere on the elevation.
- SightLine funds model-home hardware and comps the model-home Shield plan.

## Why it sells homes
1. Visible security plus a lighting amenity, with zero visible cameras. The roofline looks like architectural lighting because it is.
2. The privacy story wins HOA-heavy communities: nothing on the elevation to approve, masks enforced on-device at setup, audio off by default, no face databases, Core plan has no cloud path at all.
3. Verified response, not false alarms: only AVS-01 score >= 2 video-verified events are forwarded (94-98% of industry dispatches are false; this system was built to not be that).
4. Insurance angle at the sales table: carriers are funding prevention (State Farm/ADT), and verified-coverage discount programs of 5-15% (est.) are the emerging norm.

## Pilot structure
- One community, 20-50 lots, one model-home rig.
- Success metrics: attach rate at closing, plan conversion at 90 days, callback rate, buyer NPS on the amenity.
- Expansion: per-division pricing at 500+ lots/year commitment.
