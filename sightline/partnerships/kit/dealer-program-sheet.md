# SightLine Dealer Program

One page. The numbers first.

## The money
- Ticket: $1,299-1,799 installed (est.), single-story kit.
- Your margin: ~45% (est.) on hardware + install.
- Plan rev-share, monthly, forever, by tier: Certified 20%, Pro 25%, Elite 30% of plan revenue (Plus $9.99, Shield $19.99; Core is $0 and local-only).
- Crew math: a certified 2-person crew does 3 installs/day at 2.4 job-hours each (single story). That grosses $1,350/day in install labor and keeps ~$840 (est.) after materials and overhead.

## Certification (the gate, and the moat)
- Half-day course + bench practical, graded by the QC agent (5 photo gates: rail level/spacing, gasket seated, connector clicked, node aim, controller wiring).
- Class 2 low voltage only. Never mains. No electrician license required in most jurisdictions (verify locally).
- Expected ramp: 85%+ QC first-pass rate by job 5.

## The demo that closes
The 2:14 AM story. One tap in the app: a recorded event replays on the customer's own roofline demo. Subject enters the yard at 2:14 AM, lights escalate, Guardian scores it AVS-01 level 3, subject flees, the verified dispatch payload appears on screen. No visible cameras anywhere. That is the pitch, and it sells itself off a ladder-free driveway demo.

## Territory
- First-refusal zips, by tier: Pro gets first refusal in home zip, Elite gets metro-wide first refusal plus the demo-home program.
- Leads from SightLine marketing route to the first-refusal holder first, 4-hour claim window, then cascade.

## Who does what
| SightLine handles | You handle |
|---|---|
| Quotes via roofline scan | Selling the job |
| Kits factory-cut to the home (no measuring, no cutting, no splicing) | The install (2.4 job-hours single story) |
| QC agent grading + photo gates | Passing the gates (warranty requires all gates passed) |
| Warranty backend | Local reputation and referrals |
| Monitoring + station handoff (Shield) | Homeowner walkthrough at handoff |
| Homeowner app + support | Being the trusted local face |

## Scorecard (what moves your tier)
avg install time vs 2.4h target, QC first-pass rate (85%+ by job 5 expected), 90-day callback rate (<5%), homeowner rating. Rolling 90-day windows. Full rules in the certification tier doc.

## Start
1. Apply. 2. Half-day course + bench practical. 3. First 5 jobs with QC agent coaching. 4. Rev-share begins on your first activated plan.
